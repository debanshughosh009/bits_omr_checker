Clicking an option
How to enter answers
  • One correct option: click it.
  • Multiple correct options: Shift+click (or switch to "selects several") to pick more than one. The chip on the row says what it means: all the student must mark exactly these options; any any one of them earns the marks. Click the chip to switch.
  • ★ Bonus: everyone gets the marks for that question.
  • Keyboard: click a row and type the option letter. In "sets one answer" mode it moves to the next question; Shift+letter adds an option, * bonus, Backspace clears, ↑ ↓ Enter move.
  • Quick fill: one answer per question, e.g. ABCD…, or comma separated: A/C either, A+C both, * bonus, - skip.
  • Partial credit for "all" questions can be turned on in the exam settings.
